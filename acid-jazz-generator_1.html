Time signatures tells us how beats in music are organised, i use different time signatures to change the groove and rhythmic feel rather than limiting the piece to only 4/4 as it gives a regular four beat groove, while 3/4 creates a three beat feeling, the other time signatures help make the beat feel more fresh and unpredictable, so have fun exploring! The kick drum follows the current time signature chosen by the user ad places its hit with the beats of each bar while the other elements like the snare, crash, high hat, sax build around it and create a jazzy groove
